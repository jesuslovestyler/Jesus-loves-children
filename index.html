A Bible page for little hearts

You are loved by Jesus.

He knows your name. He is glad when you come to Him. And His love for you is very, very big.

Five wonderful promises

Read them slowly. They are true for you today.

Jesus welcomes children

You never bother Jesus. He wants you close to Him.

“Allow the little children to come to me! Don’t forbid them, for God’s Kingdom belongs to such as these.”Mark 10:14 · World English Bible

Jesus loves you

God loved the whole world so much that He gave us Jesus. Everyone who trusts in Him receives life that never ends.

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.”John 3:16 · World English Bible

You are wonderfully made

God made you on purpose. There is no one else exactly like you.

“I will give thanks to you, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Your works are wonderful. My soul knows that very well.”Psalm 139:14 · World English Bible

God is always with you

At home, at school, awake or asleep—you are never alone.

“Haven’t I commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Don’t be afraid or be dismayed, for Yahweh your God is with you wherever you go.”Joshua 1:9 · World English Bible

You can talk to Jesus anytime

Prayer is talking with Jesus. Tell Him what makes you happy, what hurts, and what you need. He listens.

“Ask, and it will be given you. Seek, and you will find. Knock, and it will be opened for you.”Matthew 7:7 · World English Bible

Come to Jesus just as you are.

You do not have to be the biggest, bravest, or best. Jesus loves you, welcomes you, and will never turn away a little heart that comes to Him.

That is good news!
Bible quotations are from the World English Bible, a public-domain translation.
